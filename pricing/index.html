Good questions.

The things people ask before they pay for anything.

Will you ever lock a chapter behind Pro?

No. Every chapter, simulation and test stays free. Pro only adds things that cost money to run, like AI hints, or extras like certificates.

Do I need an account?

No. You can learn without one. A free account lets you pick up where you left off on any device.

Why are there lower prices by region?

$8 is a lot more in some countries than in others. Checkout will show a lower price where you live, and students pay half.

Is paying safe?

Yes. Payments go through a trusted payment provider, tax included, and you get a proper receipt. We never see your card.

Can I cancel?

Any time, in one click. You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for, and your progress stays with you either way. You can also get a full refund within 14 days: see the refund policy.

When does Pro open?

After sign-in and AI hints are live. Founders spots open the same day.
