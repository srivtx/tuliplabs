Pick the name.

At rest the name is one letter: a T whose stem runs on into the foot of an L, in the same little pixels as the svx mark. Below are three ways it can move when you point at it. Each one plays by itself every few seconds. Press Use this and the whole site switches, so you can live with it for a bit.

A

Spill

The TL comes apart. Its pixels fly to their places and the rest of the name pours out of the T, left to right. The pink pixel runs all the way to the end of LABS.

quiet at rest · loud on hover
B

Runner

The full name is always there. Point at it and only a pink pixel moves: it hops through every letter, lighting each one as it lands, then fades away.

the name is always readable · phones show the TL
C

Typed

TL sits on a pink keycap, like the rest of the lab. Point at it and the key goes down and types the rest of the name, with a blinking caret.

matches the keycaps on home
