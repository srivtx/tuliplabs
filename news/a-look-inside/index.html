A new face, and a look inside the lab

The home page now shows what learning here is actually like: four real moments from the tracks, played on one stage.

It is hard to tell what a course feels like from a list of chapter titles. So the home page now plays four real moments from the lab: a simulation you watch and then write, a blueprint that stacks as you build, a hidden test that fails slow code, and the garden that grows as you go.

Each moment has a few chapters you can jump straight into. The track cards became small posters in each track's colour, and moving between pages now happens behind a curtain of pink pixels.

What changed

  • Four moments on one stage, each with chapters to jump into
  • Track cards redrawn as posters
  • Page changes in pink pixels, skipped if you prefer reduced motion
