Feature · Nov 2, 2026

Every chapter now has a blueprint and a map

Each chapter shows its parts as voxel blocks that stack as you finish steps. Every course page draws a map of how its chapters wire together.

Before you write a line, you can now see what you are building. Every chapter opens with a blueprint: its parts drawn as voxel blocks, faint until you finish the step that builds them. Finish them all and the build is stamped as shipped.

The blueprint also says what the chapter builds on and what it leads to. On each course page, a map draws every chapter and the wires between them, so you can see the whole course at once.

What changed

  • A blueprint at the top of every chapter
  • Blocks stack as you finish steps
  • A map of the whole course on each track page
